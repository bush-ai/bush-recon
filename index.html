The idea

Analysis that stays honest about what it knows.

Competitive play is spatial. Recon keeps the map at the center, connects claims to timestamps, and makes uncertainty visible rather than filling gaps with fictional precision.

A reconnaissance interface

Built to make the map do the talking.

The prototype is designed for review first: explore what happened, inspect the supporting sample, then decide what the data actually means.

01 / REPLAY

Time becomes terrain.

Scrub a timestamped match sequence and watch verified player anchors change on a calibrated map.

02 / POSITIONING

Movement, with the gaps intact.

Trails show sampled anchor-to-anchor change. Missing visibility stays missing; unobserved routes are never presented as fact.

03 / TEAM SHAPE

Compare teammates in context.

Select players, reveal their shared timeline, and examine pair spacing only where both positions were independently reviewed.

04 / EVIDENCE

Findings point back to time.

Every meaningful reading is timestamp-linked, separating observed facts, derived measures, and tactical interpretation.
