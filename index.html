The prototype

One match, made explorable.

The first Recon report is a working interactive review of a Hacienda Search & Destroy clip. It makes player position, movement trails, spacing, uncertainty, and timestamp-linked findings easy to examine in one place.

  • Interactive map with player and team filters
  • Playback, movement trails, and observed-density heat
  • Pair-spacing chart based only on jointly reviewed samples
  • Clear data-quality limits within the report
